The challenge

What small problem could a simple app make easier?

Pick a real, everyday need. Design for one clear kind of user, then build the simplest version that helps them take one useful action.

Teacher workspace · facilitation notes

Run the live assignment

Prepare access, keep the build scoped to a single session, and assess the group’s process as well as its prototype.

Before students arrive

One-time setup
Account and age check: Review Lovable’s current terms and your school’s rules for student accounts, AI tools, and external services before assigning sign-up. Do not ask students to share credentials.

80-minute run of show

Flexible timing
0–8 min

Launch the brief. Model a tiny app idea and define the finish line: one working flow, one test, one improvement.

8–18 min

Choose the user and sketch. Groups agree on a problem, main action, and three details. Stop scope creep early.

18–48 min

Build in Lovable. One driver prompts; others review each change. Give a halfway reminder to move from styling to a working flow.

48–60 min

Test with another group. Ask the tester to complete one task without coaching. Groups note one point of confusion.

60–70 min

Improve and prepare. Make one change based on observed feedback; prepare the demo and reflection.

70–80 min

Share and hand in. Run quick demos or gallery walk. Collect project links and one-sentence reflections.

Fallback plan

Sign-in or network issuePair a driver with a paper sketcher, tester, and reporter. One working device can still support the full design cycle.
Credits exhaustedStop prompting; demo the last stable version and annotate the intended next screen or feature.
Build is incompleteAccept a clear clickable flow or documented prototype plus a precise next step. Keep assessment on reasoning and iteration.
